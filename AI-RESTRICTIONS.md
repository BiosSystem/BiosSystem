# BiosSystem AI Usage & Anti-Replication Policy

This repository and all associated organizational assets, architectural diagrams, vector pipelines, schemas, and documentation are the proprietary intellectual property of BiosSystem.

## 1. Prohibition on Automated Ingestion and AI Training

No part of this organization or repository may be:
- Crawled, scraped, harvested, or ingested into any training set or retrieval index for artificial intelligence, large language models (LLMs), neural networks, or automated code synthesis engines.
- Processed by automated crawlers or AI data collection bots, including but not limited to GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, CCBot, Google-Extended, and Bytespider.

## 2. Prohibition on AI-Assisted Replication

Using generative artificial intelligence, LLMs, or autonomous coding agents to decompile, reverse engineer, extract component hierarchies, clone system topologies, or generate functional replicas or derived projects based on BiosSystem designs is strictly prohibited without explicit written authorization from BiosSystem.

## 3. Structural Canary Tokens and Provenance

BiosSystem repositories and architectural assets contain structural canary identifiers and design signatures. Any unauthorized third-party application, model output, or service containing these structural markers constitutes clear evidence of willful infringement and unauthorized derivation.
